KEEP THIS HANDY

Quick guide

Compatibility is a useful starting point. Your decisions during development also matter.

01 / COMPATIBILITY

Check all four pairings

Topic × genre, genre × platform, topic × audience, and platform × audience make up the ranking. Open a result to see each value.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Adjust the three stages

The sliders are starting points for the chosen genre. Game size, features, and team skills may call for changes.

03 / VARIETY

Mix up your ideas

Repeating the same topic and genre in consecutive games can hurt results. Keep your previous games' quality in mind, too.

04 / SCALE

Grow at the right time

Medium, Large, and AAA games need more Design and Tech points. Improve your graphics and engine, then grow your team before taking on bigger projects.

TRANSPARENCY

Data and method

The ranking uses the game's compatibility tables. Each pairing earns 0 to 100 points: +++ = 100, ++ = 80, + = 60, -- = 30, --- = 0. The maximum is 400. Ties favor topic × genre, then genre × platform.
